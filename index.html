VB specials

Google Places does not provide VB shelf prices. Prices cannot be reliably collected from every retailer automatically. Verified entries in prices.json are compared when fresh (within 7 days); otherwise prices are marked unknown. Retailer search links help check current specials. No invented prices.

For automatic price updates, connect an authorised retailer feed or a scheduled GitHub Action that updates prices.json. This static page cannot independently verify live checkout prices.
